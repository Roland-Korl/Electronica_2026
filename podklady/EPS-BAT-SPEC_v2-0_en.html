Primary battery packs

Requirements for suppliers: load profiles (worst case), voltage, temperature, dimensions, connection

EPS-BAT-SPEC
Version 2.0 · 11/2026
Contact: R. Korl
Summary. We need two primary battery packs Li-SOCl2 3.6 V (bobbin cell) with a parallel hybrid pulse capacitor (HLC / SPC) for two battery-powered outdoor devices with a radio module: Pack A about 8.5 Ah (C size class) for a low-power device with 10 years of service life, and Pack B about 19 Ah (D size class) for a monitoring device with at least 3 years of service life. Both packs are standard pre-assembled packs (cell + capacitor) with wires and a JST XHP-2 connector, replaceable in the field, not potted. All load values below are a worst case with margin, based on measurements of our prototype boards and our energy model. This document replaces our earlier requests EPS-BAT-01 and EPS-BAT-02.

Self-dischargemax. 1 % per year at +25 °C

Protectionthe device has a 2 A fuse and reverse polarity protection. A fuse or PTC inside the pack is welcome if the voltage drop at 0.6 A is max. 0.1 V.

Buffer3 × 100 µF ceramic at the radio module supply (covers the transmit pulses together with the HLC / SPC)
Input voltage rangeworks from 3.0 V; max. 4.2 V at the input
Monitoringthe device measures the battery voltage under load and sends it with the data, so field behaviour can be shared with you

6 Temperature and environment

  • Operation: −40 … +70 °C ambient, pulses also at −40 °C. In a closed box in the sun the battery can get warmer: please state the behaviour up to +85 °C.
  • Storage before assembly up to 1 year: please state the recommended storage conditions and the shelf life of spare packs.
  • Outdoors in a sealed box (Pack B: metal box with a breather vent); humidity and condensation cannot be excluded.
  • Vibration and shock of an outdoor installation (Pack B is installed next to a railway track; we take EN 61373 as a framework). The pack is held in foam, any orientation.

8 Compliance and transport

  • UN 38.3 (test summary), IEC 60086-4, EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 (portable battery, replaceable in the field: CE marking, labelling, declaration of conformity)
  • Safety data sheet (SDS / MSDS); transport conditions for packs shipped alone (UN 3090) and installed in the device (UN 3091)

9 Quantities

Samples: 5 pcs of each pack version (Q1 2027); series at start 1,000 … 3,000 pcs per year per pack, with potential to grow to significantly higher volumes.
