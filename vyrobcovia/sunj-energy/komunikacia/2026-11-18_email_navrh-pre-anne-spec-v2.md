## Text e-mailu (po anglicky)

Subject: Battery packs – updated requirements EPS-BAT-SPEC v2.0 and quotation changes

Hello Anne,

Thank you very much for the quotations and the drawings.

Please find attached our updated requirements EPS-BAT-SPEC v2.0. It contains the detailed load profiles of both devices, as you asked, and it replaces our earlier requests EPS-BAT-01 and EPS-BAT-02. All values are a worst case with margin. Pack A (ER26500H + EPC1520) is for profile A, and Pack B (ER34615H + EPC1520) is for profile B.

Compared to what I wrote before, these points are new or changed:

1. Temperature: operation from -40 °C to +70 °C, and the battery can reach +85 °C in a closed box in the sun. Please tell us the behaviour up to +85 °C and the storage conditions (the EPC1520 datasheet says storage up to +60 °C).
2. Voltage: at least 3.2 V during 0.6 A / 10 ms pulses, and at least 3.3 V during the radio session (about 150 mA for up to 7 minutes), over the whole temperature range and service life.
3. Service life: Pack A at least 10 years with profile A, Pack B at least 3 years with profile B.
4. Wires: 150 ± 10 mm instead of 100 mm, with insulation rated at least +85 °C. Your drawings show UL1007 wire; as far as we know it is rated 80 °C, so please use a 105 °C type (for example UL1569 or UL1015).
5. Connector: JST XHP-2 with pin 1 = plus (red) and pin 2 = minus (black), as in your drawings. Thank you.
6. Space for Pack B: max. 65 × 50 × 38 mm and max. 120 g, so only the side-by-side version fits. Please add the thickness of the ER34615H side-by-side pack to the drawing and confirm it is max. 38 mm.
7. Both packs are replaceable in the field and placed in foam in a sealed box.

Could you please make your discharge tests according to the profiles in sections 3 and 4, and send us the results requested in section 10 (usable capacity, pulse voltage at -40 / +20 / +70 °C, voltage delay after long rest)?

For the quotations, please also update:

1. Wire length 150 mm and the 105 °C wire in all quotations and drawings.
2. Add "Connector: XH-2P" also to the ER34615H lines.
3. Mark clearly which line is the straight version and which is the side-by-side version.
4. Samples: 20 pieces in total, 5 pieces of each of the four versions (your quotation is for 40 pieces).
5. The DHL quotation for 100 pieces says DAP in the terms but DDP in the total. Please confirm which one is valid and who pays the import duty and VAT.
6. Please add prices for 5,000 pieces.
7. Please send a proforma invoice for the samples.

Thank you very much for your help!

Best regards,
Roland Korl
EPS Germany GmbH
