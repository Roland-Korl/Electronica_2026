## Návrh odpovede (po anglicky)

Subject: Battery packs – our requirements EPS-BAT-SPEC v2.0

Dear Alana,

Thank you for your e-mail and the catalog. It was a pleasure to meet you in Munich.

Please find attached our requirements EPS-BAT-SPEC v2.0. In short, we need two primary Li-SOCl2 battery packs with a hybrid pulse capacitor (HLC / SPC), pre-assembled with wires and a JST XHP-2 connector:

1. Pack A: about 8.5 Ah (C size, for example ER26500 + capacitor), 10 years of service life.
2. Pack B: about 19 Ah (D size, for example ER34615 + capacitor), at least 3 years of service life, max. 65 × 50 × 38 mm and 120 g.

The load profiles, the temperature range (-40 to +70 °C) and the pulse requirements (0.6 A / 10 ms, min. 3.2 V) are in the document.

Your catalog shows the ER26500 and ER34615 cells, but no hybrid pulse capacitor. Can you supply complete packs with the cell and the capacitor? If yes, please send us your proposal with drawings, datasheets, the usable capacity at our profiles, prices for 5 / 100 / 1,000 / 5,000 pcs, delivery terms and the lead time for samples (5 pcs of each pack version).

Best regards,
Roland Korl
E.P.S. Germany GmbH
